How it works

  1. 01

    Point it at a playlist.

    Open the playlist in YouTube Music, choose Share and copy the link. Paste it into the deck; Musico reads the tracklist once.

  2. 02

    It skips what you already have.

    Every file carries its video id. If the MP3 is already on disk, Musico leaves it alone. If it is missing or empty, it fetches it again.

  3. 03

    The folder is yours.

    Tracks land in downloads/<playlist>/ as tagged MP3s with cover art. Take them to any player; nothing checks back in.
